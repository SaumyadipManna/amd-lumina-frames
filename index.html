About AMD Lumina Frames

AMD Lumina Frames provides door and window solutions designed to complement modern residential and commercial spaces. We focus on aluminium and uPVC frames, practical product selection, accurate measurements, and installation requirements tailored to each project.

We welcome enquiries from homeowners, civil engineers, builders, contractors, and architects in Pursurah and nearby areas. Tell us what you are planning, and we can discuss suitable product options and the next steps for your project.
